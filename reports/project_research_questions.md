# Research Questions

The following questions are answerable with the existing 60-record pilot at a descriptive or methodological level. They should not be interpreted as clinical or population-level questions.

## Questions answerable with the current pilot

1. What final non-clinical linguistic-signal label sets occur in the 60-record pilot, and how often does each occur?
2. How do the descriptive label-set frequencies differ across the 20 YouTube, 20 Reddit, and 20 Twitter/X records?
3. Which context patterns—such as first-person, educational, quoted, fictional, promotional, sarcastic, or insufficient-context text—occur in the pilot?
4. Which annotation decisions require context flags or adjudication notes, and what recurring ambiguity patterns appear?
5. What data-quality and leakage risks are visible in the pilot and its linked raw-dataset audit, including duplicate text, synthetic records, and text mismatches?

These questions support frequency tables, qualitative examples, and a methods chapter. They do not support statistical significance claims, prevalence estimates, diagnosis, risk prediction, or model performance claims.

## Questions requiring a larger approved dataset

- Can a classifier generalize to unseen users, unseen posts, future posts, or unseen platforms?
- Are platform differences reproducible beyond this 20-record-per-platform pilot?
- What precision, recall, macro-F1, or calibrated performance can be achieved?
- How stable are rare labels and multi-label combinations?

Those questions require more policy-valid independent annotations, stronger agreement evidence, a documented leakage-safe grouping policy, and a frozen evaluation set.
